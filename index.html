MEMORY 01

our very first meetup on 23 June!!

This picture is from our very first meetup on 23 June!! I was so confused and nervous that day, and you already know how annoying I was. 😂 When I was standing in front of Take Out and saw you inside, I honestly thought you looked so pretty. After that, we went to Coffee Lime, had Mint Lemon, and then I got to shake hands with one of my favorite people. And when it was finally time to leave, I genuinely didn’t want to go. 🙂 It was a simple day, but I’ll always remember our first meetup.

MEMORY 02

our second meetup on 6 July !!

This picture is from our second meetup on 6 July !! We went to the Air Force Museum, and honestly, this meetup was a little different because just before coming, you got scolded by Aunty but still, we came to meet each other. It was also the first time I actually touched you, and we had so much fun together. We even went on a boat ride! After everything, we had my favorite thing milk tea. ☕️ And then, once again, we had to say goodbye and leave. 🙂

MEMORY 03

our third meetup on 15 July!!

This picture is from our third meetup on 15 July!! We first met in front of Gojo Ramen, and then went inside together. This time, the treat was from your side, and we had so much fun together! After that, we left to head towards Uttara. It was our first time riding a rickshaw together, and then we took the Metro and came to Uttara. After reaching Uttara Central, we took another rickshaw, chilled there for a long time, and for the first time, we held hands while walking for a little while. 🙂 Then I dropped you off at Shishu Mela Bridge and headed back home.

MEMORY 04

our fourth meetup on 6 August!!

This picture is from our fourth meetup on 6 August!! We originally planned to go to KFC, but after getting there, we ended up leaving and went to BFC instead. We spent so much time together that day, and you even made and brought so many things for me with so much effort. I was genuinely so happy to get them. 🥹❤️ On our way back, I got you your favorite sunflower 🌻, and this picture was taken with that flower. And honestly, you looked so beautiful that day.

MEMORY 05

our 5th meetup on 30 August!!

This was our 5th meetup, and kind of our last one for now. InshaAllah, we’ll meet again soon. This happened on 30 August. That day, you suddenly called me and asked if I could meet you because you were alone and wanted to go out. You weren’t feeling okay, so I agreed and came. I kept you waiting at Chillox for about 1 hour and 30 minutes, but when I finally arrived, we talked for a long time. You were feeling really down, and you even cried. After that, we went outside for a little walk.
